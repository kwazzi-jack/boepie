# Nonlinear Kalman filters for calibration

## Abstract

This paper proposes nonlinear Kalman-filter calibration to estimate physical RIME terms with improved robustness in low signal-to-noise and high-complexity settings. It targets regimes where large free-parameter Jones solves become fragile.
