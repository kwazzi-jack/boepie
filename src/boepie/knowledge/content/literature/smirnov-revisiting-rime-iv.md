# Revisiting RIME IV generalized tensor formalism

## Abstract

This paper generalizes RIME beyond the standard matrix formulation by using tensor algebra, enabling clear treatment of effects such as coupling and non-classical receptor arrangements. It clarifies where simpler forms remain valid.
